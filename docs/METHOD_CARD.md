# Method Card - IdeaForge AI

**Purpose:** Auditable early-stage screening of research questions and structured transition to a startup-opportunity hypothesis.

**Formal DPT layer:** a finite declared set of admissible procedures, scalarized six-component procedure-cost vectors, raw DC as the minimum cost (the infimum on that finite set), signed representational change, raw QC for compression-explanatory questions, declared normalization scales, regions, dominance/frontier, coordinate equivalence, utility, distance, and sensitivity. Representational expansion and procedure infeasibility are retained for audit but excluded from the nonnegative Discovery Plane.

**Assisted layer:** optional question generation and preliminary score suggestions. Suggestions must be reviewed and are stored as AI-assisted provenance.

**Commercialization layer:** separate five-dimension heuristic and startup studio. It is not a calibrated probability and is not part of DPT.

**Failure conditions:** unstable rankings across reasonable conventions; weak inter-rater reliability; arbitrary representation-length choices; missing external problem/customer evidence; unresolved safety, legal, IP, regulatory, privacy or ethical constraints.

**Recommended use:** compare multiple alternatives under fixed conventions, retain rejected alternatives, sensitivity-test decisions, then collect external evidence.
