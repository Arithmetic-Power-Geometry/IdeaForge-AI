# Startup Studio Guide

Startup Studio begins only after a question has entered the analyzed portfolio. It does not transform a high score into a validated company.

## Venture hypothesis fields

- **Problem:** measurable pain, cost, risk, delay, waste, unmet need, or institutional burden.
- **Proposed solution:** smallest capability that could test whether the opportunity is real.
- **Target users:** people who use the product or workflow directly.
- **Paying customer:** organization or person controlling budget/procurement.
- **Value proposition:** measurable improvement that would justify adoption.
- **Existing alternatives:** incumbent products, manual workflows, workarounds, or non-consumption.
- **Differentiation hypothesis:** why the proposed approach may perform better or complement existing alternatives.
- **Required data:** minimum data needed for a credible proof-of-concept or pilot.
- **Core technology:** minimum technical stack required for the proposed solution.
- **MVP:** smallest auditable prototype that tests the highest-risk assumption.
- **Pilot KPIs:** technical performance, user outcome, economic outcome, safety, adoption, override, and stop/go criteria as appropriate.
- **Revenue options:** subscription, implementation fee, licensing, service contract, performance-linked model, or another domain-appropriate mechanism.
- **Cost drivers:** data, compute, integration, deployment, support, compliance, and customer acquisition.
- **Partners:** domain, validation, data, implementation, channel, or institutional partners.
- **IP / regulatory / ethics:** ownership, freedom-to-operate, privacy, security, safety, regulation, bias, consent, and ethical obligations.

## Evidence gates

A promising research screen should progress through explicit evidence gates:

1. Problem evidence
2. Technical proof-of-concept
3. Customer/user evidence
4. MVP evidence
5. Controlled pilot
6. IP/regulatory/security review
7. Business-model evidence
8. Scaling evidence

The application labels investment readiness as premature until external evidence exists.
